How to read these numbers
  • Synergy: WR of the combo minus the average WR of its members. Trios with few games lean toward their 3 duos' prediction.
  • 95% CI: range where the "true" WR likely falls. Wider on fewer games; also shown on synergy and scaling.
  • Reliability: effective game volume. low < 50 ≤ medium < 400 ≤ high.
  • Stats: weighted averages over the window. Vision and drakes are per minute of game.
  • CC: crowd control inflicted, measured in-game, per minute.
  • Scaling: winrate slope by game duration. Positive means it wins more in long games.
  • "All regions": exact recomputed aggregate, not an average of regional scores.
