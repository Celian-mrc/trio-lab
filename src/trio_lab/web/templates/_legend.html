How to read these numbers
  • Synergy: WR of the combo − average of the individual WRs of its members, on the same patch window. Trios with few games are shrunk toward the prediction from their 3 duos (Bayesian shrinkage, k = 200 games-equivalent): an extreme score on 2 games doesn't dominate the ranking.
  • 95% CI: 95% Wilson confidence interval on the winrate — the range within which the "true" WR falls with 95% confidence. More games means a narrower interval; on 10 games, a 70% WR means almost nothing (CI ≈ 39-90%). A CI is also shown on synergy (Newcombe method) and scaling (slope standard error) on the detail pages: if it contains 0, the score could just be statistical noise — common while game volume stays modest.
  • Reliability: effective game volume (weighted by the 1.0/0.6/0.35 multi-patch window weights) — low < 50 ≤ medium < 400 ≤ high.
  • Stats (gold, objectives, vision): weighted averages over the window; "—" when the data doesn't exist (e.g. gold@15 if all games end before 15 min). Vision and drakes are counted per minute of game (not cumulative) so as not to favor trios with longer games.
  • CC: crowd control inflicted, measured directly in-game (seconds of `timeCCingOthers`), per minute of game — not cumulative, same reason as vision/drakes.
  • Scaling: winrate slope by game duration (5 min buckets) — positive = wins more often in long games, negative = in short games. Measured only on buckets with enough games; no value if too little data.
  • "All regions": exact aggregate of the collected platforms, recomputed — not an average of regional scores.
