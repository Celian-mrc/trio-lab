What wins games

This table ranks game factors by their real weight on winning, across all games of the window (full team of 5 roles — top/jungle/mid/adc/ support, not just jungle/mid/support), measured at 15 minutes only (nothing later — an objective taken at 25 min can't cause an advantage measured at 15 min). "Real weight": each factor is isolated from the others, so as not to confuse correlation with cause — e.g. being ahead on jungle CS is partly what builds the gold advantage itself, this calculation tries to disentangle the two rather than treating everything as equal.

How to read the numbers: each cell shows a win probability BEFORE this factor → AFTER (e.g. "49% → 81%"). "Before" = a game in the average state of this population (no particular factor); "after" = the same game with THIS factor pushed to "one standard deviation above its mean" (user feedback 2026-07-24: the previous ×N format — odds ratio — was dropped, deemed hard to read and misleading when winning/losing is ~50/50). Green = this factor helps win, red = associated with losing. Watch out for the "Behind on gold" column: the "average" there is NOT the same as in the full population (all games there are already at a deficit), so "this factor pushed by one standard deviation" means "a bit less behind than the average losing-early game", not "moving ahead".

Limitations: solo queue Emerald+ data, not scrims/pro — early trends (gold, objectives) generally transfer well, champion picks much less so. Some signals (jungle CS) are partly definitional of gold itself: these are conditional correlations (each factor isolated from the others), not guaranteed causal levers — gold, kills, and in-game execution influence each other throughout the game.

What builds this gold advantage?

The table above says "having gold predicts winning" — true, but not very useful on its own, a coach already knows that: gold isn't a lever, it's a summary of everything that has already happened. This one answers the real question: where does this advantage come from? Two stages, in the actual chronological order of a game — the DRAFT (before the game: pick strength, matchup, trio synergy) then LANING PHASE (0-15 min: jungle CS, first blood).

How to read this: each row shows the effect on gold at 15 min, all else being equal (isolated from the other factors). Continuous factors (pick strength, matchup, synergy, jungle CS, dragons, wards) are read as "for one standard deviation above the mean"; first blood and herald are direct yes/no effects.
