Flex/hybrid picks

Detects champions played in a real secondary role (not an isolated troll pick) with a resource profile that deviates from the role average — signal of a hybrid meta (e.g. a bruiser/skirmisher in support), not necessarily a data artifact.

How to read this: "secondary role" (column of the same name) = a role where this champion has ≥ 100 games and ≥ 5% of their playtime, but not their main role. "Gold@15 vs secondary role average" always compares to the SECONDARY ROLE (not the main role): their average gold at 15 min when played in this secondary role, compared to the average gold of ALL champions in that same secondary role. E.g. Twitch in Support: we compare his gold to the average of SUPPORTS, not the average of ADCs. +8% = plays more "carry" than the norm for this role; -10% = below. Only deviations ≥ 5% are shown — below that, the profile is essentially that of the role, not a real signal. "Secondary role WR" answers a different question: does this flex pick actually WIN, not just play differently — a resource profile that deviates but a WR that collapses is not the same signal as a profile that deviates AND wins as much or more than the role average.

Limitation: the gold/damage ratio only covers match_role_stats, deployed on 2026-07-19 — depth grows over time, the numbers are still young. WR comes from agg_champion (full history).
